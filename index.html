How to calculate how much concrete you need

Measure the length and width of the area, then measure the required concrete thickness. The calculator converts those measurements into volume and adds your chosen waste allowance.

Why add extra concrete?

Real projects rarely match perfect measurements. Uneven excavation, spillage and small measurement differences can increase the required volume. A modest waste allowance can help reduce the risk of ordering too little.

Concrete bags vs ready-mix

Small jobs may use premixed bags, while larger slabs are often easier to order as ready-mix by volume. Always confirm bag yield on the exact product and ordering rules with your supplier.
